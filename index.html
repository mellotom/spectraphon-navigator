dual spectral oscillator · each side: sam (input drives harmonics) or sao (plays stored arrays) · noise and chaos modes · by tom erbe
01

what it is

sp_01
two sides
A and B, mirror images · each is an oscillator in its own mode
a spectrum
a set of harmonic levels · not a recording · plays at any pitch
like a complex osc
freq, partials, fm bus, follow/sync and sine work the same in every mode

sp_02 · fig.01 · mode button

sao · lit
always oscillates · plays spectra from a stored array
noise · chaos
press on past sao (fw sp52+) · the centre LEDs show which: noise 01, chaos 10
mix them
each side has its own mode · fm one with the other either way

sam

sp_03 · spectral amplitude modulation
a-in
input level · ticks mark modular and line level
slide
where the input’s first harmonic sits · lower = brighter output
focus
band width · low = sharp, quick · high = rings, drones, even echo
sub/cv
envelope follower of the input · try it into slide or focus

04

sp_04 · spectral array oscillation

picks the spectrum: first (ccw) to last (cw) in the array
focus

[ shift ] 2 s
planar style: focus = fine, slide = coarse
clk
steps through the array · adds to slide and focus
